# Afghan Dari update v0.4

- All 75 current DMV exam questions now have hand-authored Afghan Dari question, three answer choices, and explanation text. English remains visible.
- A visible translation control appears on the mock-exam screen and persists across DMV Adventure and English/Social lesson screens.
- Translation assistance no longer reduces the XP reward in the driving adventure.
- Existing question IDs and saved exam history remain unchanged.
- These are independently authored study translations, not official DMV translations. Native Afghan Dari review is recommended before public release.
- The home dashboard and other non-lesson navigation are still English-first; this update does not translate every navigation label.
- The practice bank remains 75 questions, not a complete handbook or 600-question bank.
