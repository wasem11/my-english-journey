## Final generation prompt

Create a high-quality game environment background asset for a mobile English-learning neighborhood exploration game for an adult woman. A warm, believable California neighborhood full of life, rendered as a sophisticated semi-realistic 3D life-simulation game diorama with fine natural textures, realistic building proportions, lush detailed trees and foliage, sunny late afternoon light, delicate shadows, flower gardens, shrubs, lawns, hedges, paved sidewalks, crosswalks, bicycles, a few parked cars, tiny people strolling, a dog walker, small birds near gardens. NOT a UI mockup, NOT flat vector shapes, NOT a board game, NOT separate icons or cards, NO text, NO labels, NO numbers, NO UI. Entire canvas is one continuous coherent walkable neighborhood scene. Tall portrait composition, ideally 1024 x 2048 or similarly tall. Elevated almost top-down three-quarter view with consistent perspective, north toward top. A gently curving neighborhood street with sidewalks runs through the middle of the entire image, linking all places naturally. Buildings and grounds fill the canvas. Ten recognizable destinations in five spacious rows, positioned approximately as follows using fractions of the canvas: row1 at y=.12: preschool with a small colorful fenced playground at x=.24, brick elementary school with flagpole and basketball court at x=.76; row2 y=.30: produce market with green awning, crates of fruit and parked bicycle at x=.24, inviting terracotta-roof corner cafe with outdoor umbrellas and tables at x=.76; row3 y=.48: small modern family clinic with a medical cross symbol at x=.24, handsome public library with steps, large windows, and reading garden at x=.76; row4 y=.66: richly landscaped public park with winding walking paths, pond, benches and a large shade tree at x=.24, lovely lived-in cream stucco family house with front garden and mailbox at x=.76; row5 y=.84: town square and bus shelter with a small bus beside the curb at x=.24, welcoming community center with garden courtyard at x=.76. Distinct architecture for every location, never ten copies of the same building. Vegetation and residential filler buildings organically surround destinations, especially edges and spaces between rows. Scene must feel like a real neighborhood, abundant trees, birds, street life, human scale, comfortable lived-in detail. Palette: natural sage/forest foliage, soft grass, warm cream stucco, terracotta, slate paving; sophisticated not oversaturated. Crisp readable architectural silhouettes at mobile scale. No sky horizon. No decorative borders. Leave the street open enough for an animated walking character overlay. The painted scene itself has no floating badges or cards.
